About YIMING GLOBAL

Zhang Yiming (Xiamen) Network Technology Co., Ltd. · One-stop cross-border compliance service provider in Southeast Asia. We only do one thing: let the products and subjects of Chinese companies operate legally, compliantly and sustainably in Southeast Asia.

Our Story

YIMING GLOBAL — Born for the Sea
Be the first stop for Chinese companies in Southeast Asia

YIMING GLOBAL focuses on providing one-stop enterprise services for Chinese enterprises to land in the Southeast Asian market, with a service network covering the Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia and Singapore.

We operate in multiple locations in China and Southeast Asia. Local certified CPA accountants and lawyers form a delivery team. From company registration, fiscal and tax compliance to certification licenses, local teams are connected with government and supervision at every step, and the progress of implementation can be tracked.
